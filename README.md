# GreatApe AI skills

Agent Skills for moving your work to a GreatApe digital employee.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| [`employee-pack`](skills/employee-pack/SKILL.md) | Your assistant (Claude, ChatGPT or any other) packs what it knows about your work into `employee-pack.zip`. Upload it in your employee's first chat and say "set yourself up from this pack". |

**Install:** in Claude, add the `skills/employee-pack` folder as a skill. In any other assistant, paste `SKILL.md` into its custom instructions, or attach it and say "follow this skill".
